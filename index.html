Calendar Text Assistant

A private, invite-only service run by Roshni Bhagalia.

What this is

Calendar Text Assistant is a personal tool that lets the owner, and a small number of people the owner personally invites, add events to the owner's Google Calendar by sending a text message. For example, texting Dinner with Sam Friday 7pm creates a calendar event for that time. It is not a public or commercial service, and it is not open to sign-ups.

Who can use it

Only people the owner has personally invited, and whose phone numbers the owner has added by hand, can use the service. Texts from any other number are ignored.

How you opt in

The owner invites you directly, in person or by a personal message, and explains what the service does. You are added only after you agree. By texting the service's number you confirm you have agreed to receive its replies.

What messages you will receive

You receive only replies to texts you send. There is no marketing and no unsolicited messaging. Each text you send gets one reply, such as:

Added: "Dinner with Sam" - Fri Sep 25, 7:00-8:00 PM. Reply UNDO to remove. Couldn't find a date/time in that. Try again with more detail.

Message and data rates may apply.

Opting out

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages, or contact the owner (below) and you will be removed. For help, contact the owner.

Privacy

  • The service stores your phone number, the name the owner gave it, and a record of your most recent event so it can be undone.
  • The text of your message is sent to the following providers only to run the service: Twilio (delivers text messages) and Google (stores the calendar event).
  • Your phone number and message content are not sold, and are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
  • You can ask to be removed, and to have your stored details deleted, at any time.
